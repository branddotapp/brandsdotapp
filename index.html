Why an app

The numbers behind apps

  • 1.8×

    Higher conversion

    App sessions convert at a median 1.8x the brand’s mobile site (21 retailers, like-for-like).

    Source: Poq ↗
  • +24%

    Total revenue

    Fashion brands’ average revenue lift after launching an app, across $31.5B of Shopify orders.

    Source: Tapcart ↗
  • +9%

    Higher order value

    80% of retailers saw higher average order values in-app, with a median uplift of 9% (21 retailers, app vs mobile web).

    Source: Poq ↗
  • 74%

    Push opt-in

    UK brand AYBL got 74% of app users to opt in to push, which drives 10%+ of its revenue.

    Source: AYBL case study, Venn Apps ↗
